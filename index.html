Integrity & Standards

Project Policies & Guidelines

Clear expectations, Arizona statutory compliance, and transparent communication for every residential remodel we undertake.

01

Estimates & Project Pricing

All estimates are based on the information, plans, and project scope available at the time of preparation. Final pricing may vary due to unforeseen conditions, engineering requirements, material selections, or changes in the scope of work.

02

Payments & Agreements

All payment schedules, deposits, and project milestones will be clearly outlined in a written construction agreement. Payments are subject to applicable Arizona laws and contractual terms.

03

Changes & Additional Work

Any work outside the original agreed scope must be documented and approved through a written change order, including applicable pricing and schedule adjustments.

04

Construction Timelines

We strive to complete every project within the agreed timeframe. However, schedules may be affected by permit approvals, inspections, weather, material availability, unforeseen site conditions, or customer-requested changes.

05

Permits & Building Standards

Projects will be performed in accordance with applicable building codes, approved plans, permit requirements, and local regulations.

06

Quality & Workmanship

We are committed to professional workmanship and customer satisfaction. Applicable workmanship warranties and their terms will be provided in the construction agreement.

07

Customer Communication

We believe successful projects begin with clear communication, transparency, mutual respect, and timely decisions throughout the construction process.

08

Privacy & Customer Information

We respect our customers' privacy. Information submitted through our website will be used to respond to inquiries, prepare estimates, and provide requested services, as further described in our Privacy Policy.
